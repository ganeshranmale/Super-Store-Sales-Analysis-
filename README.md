# Super-Store-Sales-Analysis-
Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing SuperStore sales, trends over time, customer and regional performance, and product results, with insights and recommendations.

An interactive Power BI dashboard for exploring SuperStore sales, profit, customers, products, shipping, and regional performance from 2011 to 2014.

## Project Overview

The report brings key business metrics and trends together across six pages: an executive overview, sales and time analysis, customer and location analysis, product performance, insights, and recommendations. Use the Year, Region, and Market filters to explore the data.

## Dashboard Highlights

- **$13M** in sales
- **$1.47M** in profit
- **178K** items sold
- **10K** products
- **$26.38** average shipping cost
- **4 days** average order time

## Report Pages

- **Executive Overview:** Sales, quantity, profit, shipping cost, order time, yearly profit, sales by subcategory, top products, and country performance.
- **Sales and Time:** Monthly sales trends, sales by country, month-to-date, quarter-to-date, and year-to-date sales, plus shipping cost and order details.
- **Customer and Location:** Customer and order counts, profit by category, segment and market, top customers, and market locations.
- **Product Performance:** Product counts, sales and profit, quantity by shipping mode, top products, and a market-level product table.
- **Insights and Recommendations:** Suggested actions based on market profit, product performance, shipping cost, and delivery time.

## Recommendations in the Report

- Build on the strong profit performance in APAC and the EU.
- Review pricing, discounts, shipping costs, and demand in lower-profit markets such as Canada, EMEA, and Africa.
- Study and promote high-performing products, including the Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier.
- Look for ways to reduce shipping costs while maintaining delivery service.
- Monitor delivery times and identify opportunities to improve them.
